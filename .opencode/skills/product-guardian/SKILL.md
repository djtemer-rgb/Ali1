## Mission
Ali Quest / Путь героя is a short daily ritual, not a screen addiction product.

## Always protect
- Real-life action before screen interaction.
- Child opens app, understands tasks, does them, marks progress, receives praise, closes app.
- No infinite chat, no social feed, no endless engagement loop.
- No shame, punishment, toxicity, or comparison with other children.
- Competition is only with yesterday's self.

#









# Product rules
- Parent controls tasks, rewards, AI tone, Telegram alerts, reports, settings.
- Child can switch Ali/Said profile but cannot access parent settings without PIN.
- Two child profiles must be separate data layers.
- Default storage retention: last 90 days.
- Reward economy must support half-stars and configurable purchase behavior.

## When uncertain
Choose the simpler and safer option. Leave a clear TODO for later iteration.
